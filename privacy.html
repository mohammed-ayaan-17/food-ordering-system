Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 12, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order through Belagavi Kitchen Direct, we collect only the information required to prepare and deliver your meal: your full name, delivery street address, landmark details, and selected food items.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your name and address are shared solely with the assigned delivery rider and kitchen dispatch staff to calculate delivery routes and hand over your order accurately. We do not sell, rent, or distribute customer details to third-party advertisers.

3. Data Retention

Order records are retained in the active server session history for order verification, delivery confirmation, and operational audits.

4. Contact

For privacy inquiries or requests to correct order details, contact support at support@belagavikitchendirect.in.
