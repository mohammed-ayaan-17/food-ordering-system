Terms and Conditions

Effective Date: November 12, 2026

1. Order Placement and Pricing

All menu prices are listed in Indian Rupees (Rs.). Express priority dispatch adds a flat Rs. 30.00 handling fee to prioritize preparation and rider assignment ahead of standard queue orders.

2. Delivery Address Accuracy

Customers are responsible for providing an accurate street address, flat number, and landmark. Delivery riders follow the shortest computed route from the Central Kitchen Hub to the specified destination area.

3. Dispatch and Delivery Confirmation

Orders move through three states: Queued, Out for Delivery (upon rider dispatch), and Delivered (confirmed by the rider upon handover). Once an order is dispatched to a rider, modifications to the cart or destination cannot be applied.

4. Service Availability

Dispatch depends on available delivery partners. During peak demand when all riders are assigned active deliveries, orders remain safely queued in priority order until the next rider becomes available.
